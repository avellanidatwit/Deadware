View commands in the Wiki (new tab)

Tab indents four spaces; Shift+Tab unindents. Select multiple lines to indent them together. Press Escape, then Tab to leave the editor.

Use WHEN conditions and OTHERWISE with one action per rule. Combine conditions with NOT, AND, OR. Use selectors such as ATTACK weakest zombie, items such as USE bandage, and memory such as MOVE_TO home. Open the Wiki for the full command reference. Changes last until restart or refresh. For zombies, use WHEN survivorNearby with CHASE survivor, then OTHERWISE with WANDER. Add a range after either nearby condition, such as WHEN zombieNearby 10 or WHEN survivorNearby 8; omit it for the default of 5 tiles. Zombies also support MOVE, EXPLORE, WAIT, and health conditions.
