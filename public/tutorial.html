Getting started / 02

Your first survivor program

Write a rule. Watch a decision. Adapt your strategy.

1. Pause and observe

Open the Game and press Pause. Find your survivor, check their health, and look for nearby zombies. Step advances the world one update at a time.

2. Write a priority list

Select the survivor in the editor and try this script. This script approaches visible closed doors, opens them, and explores the revealed interior. It only scavenges when inventory has space. Each condition has one action underneath it. The first matching rule wins.

WHEN health < 40 AND hasItem bandage
    USE bandage

WHEN zombieNearby 1
    ATTACK zombie

WHEN zombieNearby 6
    MOVE_AWAY nearest zombie

WHEN thirsty AND hasItem water
    USE water

WHEN hungry AND hasItem food
    EAT food

WHEN hasItem weapon AND NOT equipped weapon
    EQUIP weapon

WHEN itemsOnFloor AND inventorySpace
    PICK_UP items

WHEN containerNearby AND inventorySpace
    SEARCH nearest container

WHEN doorNearby
    OPEN door

WHEN containerNearby 8 AND inventorySpace
    MOVE_TO nearest container

WHEN distance nearest door > 1
    MOVE_TO nearest door

OTHERWISE
    EXPLORE

3. Apply, then step

Press Apply script, then Step. Read Latest action to see what happened. Survivor movement happens every second update; on other updates the script can select a later interaction. A failed action still takes an update.

4. Give needs a priority

The starter script places these rules above exploration and scavenging. Your survivor needs to find and carry the matching items first.

WHEN health < 40 AND hasItem bandage
    USE bandage

WHEN thirsty AND hasItem water
    USE water

WHEN hungry AND hasItem food
    EAT food
