Account

Your programs keep running while you are offline.

Connect to your account

Log in on the Game page with your email and password. Your server operator provides your account. On the first local startup, the server prints the initial operator email and password.

Deployed scripts and world state are saved on the server. After login, select one of your survivors or zombies to view its surroundings and status. Use Log out to end your session. Keep your password private. Self-service registration and account recovery are not available yet.
